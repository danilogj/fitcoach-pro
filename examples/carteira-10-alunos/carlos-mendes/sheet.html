Rules that matter more than exercise selection

Amber RIR (1-2) — heavy compounds and pull-ups. Hold back. Failure here costs the whole week.

Green RIR (0-1) — isolation, machines and cables. Go to failure freely.

Progression. Hit the top of the rep range on every sets while holding the target RIR → add load next session. Not before.

Warm-up ramp on heavy compounds: 50% · 70% · 85%, 3-5 reps.

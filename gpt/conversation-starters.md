# Conversation starters

Paste each line into one of the four "Conversation starters" fields in the GPT Builder.

```
New client — walk me through the intake
Build a block: 3 days, dumbbells and cables only, intermediate
Analyze this log and tell me what to adjust
Weekly check-in: all sessions done, recovery poor
```

These four cover the highest-frequency workflows and teach the expected input format — the second one in particular signals that days, equipment and profile are mandatory.

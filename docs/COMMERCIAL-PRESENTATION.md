## 💎 The 4 Pillars that Delight Clients & Scale Your Coaching

![Personal Trainer Workflow](assets/trainer-workflow.jpg)

### 1. 🎯 Biomechanical Precision & True Volume Audit
No more guessing if weekly volume is effective. FitCoach Pro audits each muscle group against **MEV (Minimum Effective Volume)**, **MAV (Optimal Volume)**, and **MRV (Maximum Recoverable Volume)** landmarks.

### 2. 🛡️ Joint Safety & Spinal Protection
The leading cause of client dropout is joint pain. The engine tracks **Axial Load**, manages spinal stress, and suggests chest-supported substitutions for shoulder, lower back, knee, and wrist limitations.

### 3. 📱 The Pocket Experience on Your Client's Phone (`sheet.html`)
Your client opens a sleek, fast, offline web app on their phone with an active rest timer, **RIR (Reps in Reserve)** targets, and previous session loads for direct comparison.

### 4. 🧠 Data-Driven Check-Ins: Science Over Guesswork
Did a client plateau? The system evaluates weekly logs, analyzes weight trends, and provides **one specific diagnosis and adjustment** (e.g., *+200 kcal adjustment*, *20% volume reduction*, or *planned deload*).

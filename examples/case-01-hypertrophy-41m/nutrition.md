### Decision rules

1. **Weight flat for 3 weeks** (weekly averages) → add 200 kcal.
2. **Fat mass past 14.5 kg before 75 kg body weight** → cut 200 kcal.
3. **Weight climbing above 0.4 kg/week consistently** → cut 200 kcal.
4. **On reaching 78 kg** → maintenance rose ~100 kcal from the new mass alone. Add 200 kcal to keep progressing, or hold at 2,700 to stabilize.

## Measurement hierarchy

1. Training log — load × reps × RIR
2. Weight, 7-day average — daily, same time, fasted, post-void
3. Waist, weekly, at the navel
4. Photo every 4 weeks
5. Bioimpedance every 4 weeks, never weekly

The combination that decides is **weight average + waist + training log**. If all three agree, bioimpedance is unnecessary. If they disagree, it will not settle it either — adjusting 200 kcal and waiting three weeks will.
